Back-to-School Letter to Parents From an English Teacher

The first letter home sets the tone for the whole year, and it's hard to get right when you're busy preparing your classroom. Copy the prompt below into any AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), change the parts in [brackets], and you'll get a warm, clear first draft in minutes.

The prompt

Act as an approachable, highly qualified classroom teacher welcoming a new group of [Target Grade Level/Age] students and their families at the start of the academic year. Write a 3-paragraph introduction letter that outlines your professional teaching philosophy, sets a warm and high-expectation tone for the classroom environment, and details the best way for parents to get in touch with you throughout the year. Keep the language clear, welcoming, and reassuring.

Why it works

The prompt gives the letter a simple three-part shape: who you are as a teacher, what the class will feel like, and how to reach you. Parents get the answers they care about most, and students meet a teacher who is friendly but expects effort.

What to do with the result

Add your real details: your name, your school email, the best times to reach you, and how quickly you usually reply. Then change one or two sentences so they sound like you. Send it home in the first week, or share it on your class page.

A mistake to avoid

Leaving the teaching philosophy generic. The AI doesn't know what you believe, so a sentence like "I believe every student can succeed" can sound like any teacher's letter. Replace it with one sentence in your own words about what you really care about in English class.
