Can AI write an IEP?

AI can help with drafting wording and organizing your ideas, but an IEP is a formal document that must be developed by the IEP team together with the family, based on real evaluation data. Use the prompts as a drafting aid and review everything against the student's actual needs and your school's requirements.

Is it safe to put student information into an AI chatbot?

Avoid entering student names or other identifying details. Describe the situation in general terms, and check your school or district policy on AI tools first.
