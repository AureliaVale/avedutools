Substitute Teacher Plan Template for English Class

Leaving a plan for a substitute shouldn't take your whole evening. Copy the prompt below into any AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), change the parts in [brackets], and you'll get a clear, ready-to-print plan in minutes.

The prompt

Act as a meticulous, highly organized classroom manager leaving instructions for a substitute teacher taking over your [Target Grade Level/Age] class for the day. Generate a clean, 4-step handover framework: 1. Class Roster & Dynamics: Notes on general behavior and helpful students. 2. The Main Task: Clear instructions on the exact lesson material to cover (referencing the workbook pages or digital files). 3. The Emergency Plan: What to do if the class finishes early (e.g., use a specific zero-prep game). 4. The Feedback Line: A blank section left for the substitute to write down comments regarding behavior and attendance.

Why it works

The prompt asks for four separate parts, so the substitute knows who the class is, what to teach, what to do if time is left, and where to report back. Nothing is left to guess.

What to do with the result

Add your real page numbers and file names, print the plan, and leave it on your desk where the substitute will find it. Keep a copy on your computer so you can reuse it next time.

A mistake to avoid

Writing "continue with the lesson". A substitute needs exact pages, exact instructions and a clear plan for early finishers.
